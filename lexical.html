Privacy Policy

Lexical: Word Grid Puzzle  ·  com.ameb.lexical  ·  AMEB

Last updated: 17 July 2026

The short version. Lexical has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you personally. Your game progress stays on your device and is never sent to us. The game does use two third-party services: Google AdMob (to show ads) and Google Firebase Analytics (to understand how the game is used). Those services collect some data. Everything below explains exactly what, and how to opt out.

1. Who we are

Lexical: Word Grid Puzzle ("the game", "the app") is developed and published by AMEB ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to data when you play the game. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.ameb.lexical, and to no other product.

2. We do not collect personal data

To be specific and unambiguous:

  • There are no user accounts and no sign-in. You cannot create an account, and we never ask you to.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, photos, contacts, calendar, microphone or camera input.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not ask for, or receive, any payment card details. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (see section 6).
  • We do not sell your data. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

The data described in sections 3 and 4 is collected by Google's advertising and analytics services running inside the app, not by an AMEB server.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) — Advertising ID collected and shared

The game is free and is supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. You can remove ads permanently with a one-time purchase (see section 6).

What is collected

To serve and measure ads, AdMob collects and shares your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier that Google Play Services assigns to your device) along with technical information such as your device type, operating system version, coarse location inferred from your IP address, and your interactions with ads.

Purpose: Advertising (including personalised or non-personalised ad selection, ad delivery, and frequency capping) and Analytics (ad performance measurement, and fraud prevention).

Shared: Yes. The Advertising ID and associated ad-request data are transmitted to Google and, where applicable, to Google's advertising partners.

Encrypted in transit: Yes.

Where the ads appear

For transparency, because it is part of how the game treats you:

  • Banner ads appear on menu screens and the results screen only. Never during play, and never over the grid.
  • A full-screen (interstitial) ad is not shown on the run-over screen itself. One may appear only if you decline the optional offer to continue a jammed run, and only after your first several runs. Never during play.
  • Rewarded ads are entirely optional. They are only ever shown if you choose to watch one: for an extra undo, to continue a jammed run by clearing three stuck tiles, to double a run's coins, to add an extra golden cell, or to double the daily reward. Nothing in the game is gated behind watching one.

Your choices

Google's use of the Advertising ID, and your options for controlling it, are described in Google's own policy. Please read:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (Google's partner policy)
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & AdSense policies

How to opt out or reset your Advertising ID

You are in control of this identifier at the Android system level, independently of our app. On most Android devices:

  • To reset it: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Google → All services → Ads) and tap Reset advertising ID. This assigns a new ID and disconnects your device from previously collected ad data.
  • To opt out of personalised ads: in the same screen, enable Opt out of Ads Personalisation (on newer Android versions: Delete advertising ID, which stops apps from receiving it at all). You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your advertising profile.
  • You can also review your Google-wide ad settings at My Ad Center.

Menu paths vary between manufacturers and Android versions; if the paths above do not match your device, search your Settings app for "Ads" or "Advertising ID".

4. Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used in aggregate: which grid sizes people play, how runs end, and whether a screen is crashing. This is how we decide what to fix and what to build.

What is collected: App activity (in-game events such as a run starting or ending, the grid size played, and screens viewed) and diagnostics (crash and performance data, device model, OS version, app version, country, and a Firebase-generated app instance identifier).

Purpose: Analytics. Specifically, understanding usage, diagnosing crashes, and improving the game.

Shared: No. This data is not shared with third parties beyond Google acting as our analytics processor, and is not sold.

Encrypted in transit: Yes.

We do not use this data to identify you, and we do not attempt to link it to your identity. Firebase Analytics is documented at Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Your saved game stays on your device

Your progress (runs played, best scores, coins, and unlocked themes and tile skins) is written to a single file in the app's private storage on your device (user://save.json).

  • This file is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no cloud save and no cross-device sync.
  • Because it lives in the app's private storage, uninstalling the game deletes it, and your progress is gone permanently. There is no way for us to restore it, because we never had a copy.

6. Purchases

Lexical offers optional in-app purchases (removing ads, coin packs, and a starter bundle). All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment card number or billing address. Google confirms to the app only that a purchase succeeded. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Coins in Lexical buy cosmetic items only. There is no premium currency, no paywall, and no gameplay content that can only be obtained by paying.

7. Notifications

The game can send you occasional local reminders that a grid is waiting. These are generated on your device. They are not push notifications, no message is sent from a server, and no data leaves your device to produce them. Android will ask your permission before the first one is shown, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Apps → Lexical → Notifications, or from within the game's own Settings screen.

9. Children

Lexical is rated Everyone for content, but it is not directed to children. It is intended for players aged 13 and over, and it is not part of the Google Play Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 12, although, as section 2 explains, the app has no accounts and collects no personal information from anyone.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Advertising ID: reset it, or opt out of ad personalisation, at any time using the Android settings described in section 3. This is the most direct control you have over the data this app causes to be collected.
  • Analytics: uninstalling the app stops all further analytics collection.
  • Saved progress: uninstalling the app permanently deletes it from your device.
  • Data deletion requests: because the app has no accounts and we hold no personal data that identifies you, we have no user record to look up or delete on request. There is nothing tied to your identity for us to erase. Data held by Google in connection with your Advertising ID is controlled through your Google account and the Android settings above, and through Google My Activity.
  • You may still contact us with any privacy question or concern at the address in section 12.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play listing. This policy has no version history prior to the date shown, as this is its first publication.
