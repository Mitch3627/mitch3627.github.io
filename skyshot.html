Skyshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Skyshot is published by AMEB. This policy explains what the game collects and what it does not.

What we do not collect

Skyshot has no accounts, no sign-in and no profiles. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personally identifying information.

Game progress

Your level progress, score, coins, cosmetics and settings are saved only on your own device. They are not uploaded to us and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Skyshot shows ads through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob collects and shares your device's Advertising ID along with basic device and ad-interaction information, and uses it to select and measure advertising. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose whether to watch one for an extra hint. Purchasing "Remove Ads" or the starter bundle stops banner and interstitial ads.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand which levels players reach and to receive crash reports. These record app events and device model / OS version. They do not record anything that identifies you personally.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

Skyshot is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data in transit

All data sent by the advertising, analytics and billing services above is encrypted in transit.
