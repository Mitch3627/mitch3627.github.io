Emberforge Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026 · App: Emberforge Idle (com.ameb.emberforgeidle) · Developer: AMEB

Summary

Emberforge Idle does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and share your device's Advertising ID and coarse technical data (device model, IP-derived region) to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data usage.

An optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") removes interstitial and banner advertising.

Analytics

This version of the game does not include a separate analytics SDK. We do not collect gameplay events. The only usage and diagnostic data leaving your device is what the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK gathers to serve and measure ads, described above.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

Game data

Your save (progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it. It is not uploaded and we cannot recover it.

Children

The game is rated Everyone but is targeted at players 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service, contact us and we will help address it.

Data security & sharing

Data handled by Google services (Advertising ID, analytics events) is encrypted in transit. We do not sell personal data and share it only with the Google services named above.

Your choices

  • Reset/delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings.
  • Remove ads entirely via the in-app purchase.
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app.
